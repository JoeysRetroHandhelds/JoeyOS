Dock system icons

The system icons you can choose for dock tiles (App Drawer, Start on an app, Dock icon) come from
RetroArch's menu themes: Systematic, Automatic, Monochrome, FlatUX, Retrosystem, Daite, FlatUI and
Pixel.

Source: https://github.com/libretro/retroarch-assets (the xmb folder)
Authors: the libretro team and the contributors of each theme
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

JoeyOS doesn't include the icons. Each one is downloaded from that repository when you choose it,
unchanged except for being resized to fit the tile, and kept on your device.

Console and company names and designs belong to their owners; the icons depict the systems only so
you can recognise them.
